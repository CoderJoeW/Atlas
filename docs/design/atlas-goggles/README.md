# Atlas Goggles

Headgear that floats a readout beside the Atlas block the wearer looks at. It replaced the Atlas
Wrench and its inspector dialog.

## Look

Two blocky square lens housings on a bridge, in the shared Atlas language: charcoal armoured metal,
brushed steel rims with a bolt in each corner, and amber glass lenses (`#FFB030`) glowing from within.
A dark rubberised strap with steel rivets goes around the head. Amber matches the item's gold name and
the power system's glow.

## Art pipeline

Images are generated by hand in Nano Banana Pro (1:1, 2K). The style reference is
`docs/design/small-solar-panel/references/power-system-reactive-states.jpg`. Sources are kept in
`raw/`, downscaled to 1024 px.

The icon is rendered on flat `#FF00FF` and chroma-keyed to a 512×512 RGBA PNG. The keyer takes alpha
from each pixel's distance to the sampled backdrop, un-mixes the backdrop from soft edges, despills
again after a premultiplied resize, and keeps only the largest blob. The last despill matters: without
it, the resize leaves a purple fringe on the outline.

| Asset | Source | Pack path |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory icon | `raw/icon.jpeg` | `textures/item/custom/atlas_goggles.png` |
| Housing | `raw/housing.jpeg` | `textures/item/custom/atlas_goggles_housing.png` |
| Lens | `raw/lens.jpeg` | `textures/item/custom/atlas_goggles_lens.png` |
| Strap | `raw/strap.jpeg` | `textures/item/custom/atlas_goggles_strap.png` |

The housing came out at a mean luminance of 40, darker than the icon's lit housing faces (about 65).
Minecraft shades a model's side faces to 60-80%, so at 40 the worn goggles would read as near-black.
It was brightened with a single gain (×1.59, mean 64, nothing clipped), which keeps its contrast and
cool tint.

The lens bezel has stepped, chamfered corners, and the generator filled the space outside them
inconsistently: dark grey at the top and white at the bottom. Those four corners were flood-filled,
grown 3px to take the JPEG fringe with them, and cut to hard transparency. The lens is meant to be
a thin plate in front of the housing, so the housing shows through the cuts as a chamfer, the way the
bezel corners look in the icon.

The strap generation pasted a piece of the reference image's lens housing into its lower half, and its
rivet rows came out unevenly spaced. The model only uses a thin band across one rivet row, so no
regeneration was needed: the texture is the clean top-left 1280×1280 of the 2K source (checked free of
amber). It already sat at the target brightness (mean 55), so the gain was only 1.01.

## Model

`build_model.py` writes the worn model and the item definition, and `render_goggles.py` renders the
model textured on a stand-in Steve head (`renders/worn.png`).

A head-slot item with no equipment asset draws its own item model on the head, so the one blocky model
serves the head, the hand, the ground and item frames. The item definition selects on
`display_context`: the gui gets the flat icon, and everything else gets the 3D model.

The head fills x/y/z 1.6..14.4 of model space, and the front of the model (north, low z) faces forward,
as a carved pumpkin's face does. Two 5×5×2 housings are centred on Steve's eyes (y 7.2, x 4.8 and 11.2).
Each has a 0.25-thick lens plate in front, and a bridge joins the two. Clasps at the temples join a
2-unit strap that runs round the sides and back just outside the head. Each face samples a UV span
equal to its size, so the material keeps one scale everywhere.

The item is a plain Paper item whose `item_model` is `atlas:atlas_goggles`. The item definition and
models are hand-placed in the pack, not generated by CraftEngine, and `GogglesAssetsTest` walks the
whole chain.

**Unverified until seen in game:** that the front of the model faces forward on the head (if it
doesn't, set `display.head.rotation`), and the hand, ground and item-frame transforms.
